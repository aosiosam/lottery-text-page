# QR layout update — 2026-11-16

New generations place the QR at the bottom left (x=0,y=100). Size setting is 12.5, half of the previous 25. The previous integer module scale is halved before the final pixel boundary is rounded, so width and height each equal half of their previous dimensions (nearest pixel). Black QR modules are composited at 50% opacity onto the background. Downloaded PNG pixels use the same rendering as the preview. This replaces the earlier transparent-white compensation method at the user's explicit request. Old saved composites are preserved; regenerate to obtain the new appearance.
